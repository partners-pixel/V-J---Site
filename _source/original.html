FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is corporate tax in India? +
Corporate tax in India refers to the income tax levied on the profits earned by companies. It applies to both domestic and foreign companies operating in India, subject to applicable provisions.
Who is required to pay corporate income tax? +
All companies registered in India, as well as foreign companies earning income in India, are required to comply with corporate income tax provisions.
What is the difference between corporate tax and income tax? +
Corporate tax applies to companies, while income tax applies to individuals, HUFs, and other non-corporate entities. The compliance requirements and tax treatment differ accordingly.
What is ITR-6 and who should file it? +
ITR-6 is the income tax return form applicable to companies, except those claiming exemption under specific provisions. It includes reporting of income, deductions, and tax computation.
What are advance tax obligations for companies? +
Companies are required to estimate and pay their tax liability in instalments during the financial year. This helps avoid interest and ensures timely compliance.
How does corporate tax planning help businesses? +
Corporate tax planning helps businesses structure transactions and operations efficiently, reduce tax exposure within the legal framework, and improve overall financial outcomes.
What is transfer pricing and why is it important? +
Transfer pricing refers to pricing of transactions between related entities. It must comply with arm's length principles to avoid adjustments, penalties, and disputes with tax authorities.
How are mergers and acquisitions taxed? +
Mergers and acquisitions may trigger tax implications such as capital gains, carry-forward of losses, and restructuring considerations. Proper structuring is essential to manage tax impact.
What are common corporate tax compliance requirements? +
Key requirements include maintaining books of accounts, computing taxable income, filing returns, paying advance tax, and complying with audit and reporting provisions.
What happens if a company fails to comply with corporate tax laws? +
Non-compliance may result in penalties, interest, scrutiny assessments, and legal proceedings. It may also affect the company's financial credibility and operations.
Can companies carry forward losses? +
Yes, companies can carry forward certain business losses and set them off against future profits, subject to conditions and proper filing of returns.
What is international corporate taxation? +
International corporate taxation deals with tax implications of cross-border transactions, including treaty benefits, withholding tax, and multi-jurisdictional compliance.
How do DTAA provisions apply to companies? +
Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA) help companies avoid being taxed twice on the same income by allowing relief through tax credits or exemptions.
What documents are required for corporate tax compliance? +
Typical documents include financial statements, ledgers, invoices, bank statements, prior returns, and supporting records for deductions and transactions.
When should a business engage a corporate tax advisor? +
A corporate tax advisor should be engaged during business setup, expansion, restructuring, cross-border transactions, or whenever tax complexity increases.

GST Fundamentals

GST Basics & Structure

Understanding the GST framework is essential for every business operating in India.

📌

What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax is a unified indirect tax replacing VAT, Service Tax, and Excise Duty. It follows a dual structure — CGST (Central) and SGST (State) for intra-state, and IGST for inter-state supplies.

📊

GST Rate Slabs

GST applies at 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% depending on the goods or services. Special rates apply to gold (3%), rough diamonds (0.25%), and composition dealers.

🏢

Registration Thresholds

Mandatory registration for businesses with aggregate turnover exceeding ₹40 lakhs (goods) or ₹20 lakhs (services). Special category states have a ₹10 lakh threshold.

🧾

Tax Invoice Requirements

Every registered supplier must issue a tax invoice with GSTIN, HSN/SAC code, tax amount, and place of supply. E-invoicing is mandatory for businesses with turnover above ₹5 crore.

🔄

Reverse Charge Mechanism

Under RCM, the recipient pays GST instead of the supplier. Applicable on specified goods/services like legal services, GTA, import of services, and purchases from unregistered dealers in certain cases.

🌐

Place of Supply Rules

Place of supply determines whether a transaction is intra-state or inter-state. Correct determination is critical for applying the right tax (CGST+SGST vs IGST) and avoiding disputes.

Input Tax Credit

ITC Rules & Restrictions

Input Tax Credit allows businesses to reduce their GST liability by the tax paid on purchases. Understanding eligibility and restrictions is critical to avoid scrutiny.

✦
Eligible ITC
Goods/services used for business purposes, capital goods, inputs used in making taxable supplies.
✦
Blocked Credits (Section 17(5))
Motor vehicles (exceptions apply), food/beverages, club memberships, works contract for immovable property, personal use items.
✦
GSTR-2B Matching
ITC can only be claimed if reflected in GSTR-2B. Reconciliation with purchase register is essential before filing GSTR-3B.
✦
Time Limit
ITC must be claimed by 30th November of the following financial year or date of annual return filing, whichever is earlier.

RERA Overview

Understanding RERA

The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA) was enacted to bring transparency, accountability, and efficiency to the real estate sector. It protects homebuyers' interests and establishes a regulatory authority in each state.

📌

Who Must Register

Promoters with projects having plot area >500 sq.m. or more than 8 apartments must register before advertising, selling, or booking.

📅

Registration Process

Application to state RERA authority with project details, land title, approvals, financial projections, and promoter details. Gujarat RERA operates at gujrera.gujarat.gov.in.

💰

Escrow Account

70% of funds received from allottees must be deposited in a separate escrow account and used only for land and construction costs of that project.

📋

Agent Registration

Real estate agents must register with RERA before facilitating any sale or purchase of registered projects. Separate registration for each state.

"RERA has fundamentally changed how real estate projects must be managed — compliance is not optional; it is the foundation of every project's legal standing."

V J Desai & Co. LLP — RERA Advisory
Promoter Obligations

Key Compliance Requirements

🗓️

Quarterly Updates

Promoters must update the RERA portal every quarter with construction progress, approvals obtained, and funds utilised — within 15 days of quarter-end.

📄

Annual Financial Disclosures

Audited financial statements, CA-certified statements of funds received and utilised must be uploaded annually for each registered project.

🔄

Project Extension

Extension beyond the registered completion date requires application to RERA with valid reasons. Force majeure grounds are available but require documentation.

📢

Advertisement Compliance

All advertisements must include the RERA registration number. Misrepresentation in ads is an offence under Section 89 of RERA.

🤝

Agreement for Sale

Promoters must execute a registered Agreement for Sale before receiving more than 10% of the unit price. Template prescribed by state authority must be followed.

🏗️

Structural Defect Liability

Promoters are liable for structural defects for 5 years after possession. Any defect must be rectified within 30 days of being brought to notice.

Double Tax Avoidance

DTAA Framework

India has signed DTAA with 90+ countries to prevent double taxation of the same income. DTAA provides either exemption or reduced tax rates on specific income types.

📋

Key DTAA Benefits

Reduced withholding tax on dividends, royalties, interest, and fees for technical services. Capital gains exemption in certain treaties (e.g., Mauritius, Singapore — now restricted).

📄

Tax Residency Certificate

TRC from the home country is mandatory to claim DTAA benefits. India also requires Form 10F and additional information per Section 90(5).

🏢

Permanent Establishment

PE risk arises when a foreign company has a fixed place of business in India. PE triggers Indian tax liability on attributable profits.

⚖️

Principal Purpose Test

MLI provisions now apply PPT to India's tax treaties. Arrangements designed primarily for tax benefits may be denied treaty protection.

"DTAA planning requires careful analysis of residency, PE risk, beneficial ownership, and the Principal Purpose Test — treaty shopping is no longer a viable strategy."

V J Desai & Co. LLP — International Tax
Transfer Pricing

Transfer Pricing Compliance

📊

Arm's Length Price

All transactions between associated enterprises must be at arm's length. Methods include CUP, RPM, CPM, TNMM, and Profit Split Method.

📋

Form 3CEB

Mandatory CA certification in Form 3CEB for all international transactions with associated enterprises. Due date: 31st October of the assessment year.

📁

Master File & CbCR

MNEs with consolidated revenue > ₹500 crore must maintain Master File. CbCR applies to Indian parent MNEs with consolidated revenue > ₹5,500 crore.

⚠️

TP Penalties

100-300% penalty on tax determined on TP adjustments. 2% penalty on transaction value for non-maintenance of documentation.

🤝

Advance Pricing Agreement

APA provides certainty on TP methodology for 5 years (unilateral) or bilaterally with treaty partner countries to avoid future disputes.

🌐

Safe Harbour Rules

Safe harbour rates available for specified transactions like software development, IT-enabled services, and intra-group loans to reduce TP litigation risk.

Foreign Direct Investment

FDI Compliance Framework

📋

Automatic Route

Most sectors allow FDI up to 100% under automatic route without prior government approval. Industries include manufacturing, IT, infrastructure, and services.

🏛️

Approval Route

Certain sectors like defence (>74%), media, and multi-brand retail require prior FIPB/Government approval. Application through DPIIT.

📄

FC-GPR Filing

Form FC-GPR must be filed on the RBI's FIRMS portal within 30 days of allotment of shares to foreign investors. Required for both equity and compulsorily convertible instruments.

🔄

FC-TRS Filing

Required when shares are transferred between residents and non-residents. Filed by the transferee/transferor within 60 days of receipt of consideration.

📊

Valuation Requirements

Shares issued to or transferred from non-residents must be at SEBI-registered merchant banker valuation (DCF or comparable method).

⚠️

FEMA Violations

Contravention of FEMA provisions attracts penalty up to 3x the amount involved or ₹2 lakh (whichever is higher), plus compounding proceedings.

External Commercial Borrowings

ECB Guidelines

✦
ECB Framework
RBI's ECB framework permits Indian companies to borrow from overseas lenders. Available under automatic and approval routes with end-use restrictions.
✦
Eligible Borrowers
Companies, LLPs, NBFCs, microfinance institutions, and other eligible entities as per RBI guidelines can raise ECB for permitted end-uses.
✦
Minimum Average Maturity
3 years for ECB up to $50 million. 5 years for amounts above $50 million. Infrastructure projects may have different MAM requirements.
✦
LRN & Reporting
Loan Registration Number (LRN) must be obtained before drawdown. Monthly ECB-2 returns filed with RBI through designated AD bank.

"ECB can be an effective low-cost funding source for Indian businesses, but requires careful structuring to comply with end-use restrictions, hedging requirements, and RBI reporting timelines."

V J Desai & Co. LLP — FEMA Advisory
Export Import Policy

EXIM Policy Key Schemes

📦

Advance Authorisation

Allows duty-free import of inputs used in manufacture of export products. Export obligation must be fulfilled within 18 months of issue.

🏭

EPCG Scheme

Export Promotion Capital Goods scheme allows import of capital goods at zero customs duty. Export obligation is 6x of duty saved within 6 years.

🔄

Deemed Exports

Supply to EOU, SEZ, or government projects qualifies as deemed exports. Benefits include advance authorisation and TED refund.

🏢

Export Oriented Units

EOUs can import goods duty-free. Must export 100% of production (with domestic tariff area sales allowed up to 50% of turnover).

🌐

SEIS & MEIS

Service Export from India Scheme provides duty credit scrips for service exporters. Merchandise Exports replaced by RoDTEP scheme for goods exporters.

📋

DGFT IEC

Importer Exporter Code (IEC) from DGFT is mandatory for any import/export transaction. Online application with PAN and bank details.

Audit Types

Types of Audit in India

Every audit type serves a distinct purpose — understanding which applies to your business is the first step to compliance.

⚖️

Statutory Audit

Mandatory under Companies Act 2013 for all registered companies. An independent CA examines financial statements and expresses an opinion on whether they present a true and fair view. Governed by Standards on Auditing issued by ICAI.

📋

Tax Audit (Section 44AB)

Mandatory for businesses with turnover exceeding ₹1 crore (₹10 crore if 95%+ digital) and professionals with gross receipts above ₹50 lakh. CA certifies Form 3CA/3CB and the detailed Form 3CD. Due by 30th September.

🔍

Internal Audit

Mandatory under Section 138 for listed companies, unlisted public companies above specified thresholds, and private companies with turnover above ₹200 crore or outstanding loans above ₹100 crore. Risk-based; focuses on operational controls and governance.

✅

Compliance Audit

A multi-law review covering Companies Act, GST, Income Tax, Labour Laws, FEMA, and RERA obligations. Identifies compliance gaps before regulators do. Often paired with secretarial audit (Form MR-3) for listed and specified unlisted companies.

📦

Stock Audit

Physical verification of inventories for banks, NBFCs, or management purposes. Certifies stock quantities, valuation, and quality. Mandatory for entities with working capital credit limits above bank-prescribed thresholds. Conducted periodically by empanelled CAs.

🏦

Bank / Concurrent Audit

Audit of banking operations, financial statements, and regulatory compliance. Concurrent audit runs alongside daily bank operations to ensure real-time transaction accuracy, limit compliance, and KYC adherence as per RBI guidelines.

🏛️

Government / PSU Audit

Audit of government entities, public sector undertakings, NGOs receiving grants, and cooperative societies. Follows CAG-prescribed standards. Focuses on fund utilisation, tendering compliance, and efficiency of government expenditure.

💰

Cost Audit

Mandatory for companies in specified industries (cement, pharma, telecom, etc.) with turnover above prescribed limits. CA (Cost) or CMA certifies cost records in Form CRA-3. Ensures cost accounting records are maintained correctly.

🔐

Information Systems Audit

Review of IT systems, cybersecurity controls, data integrity, and access management. Increasingly relevant as financial reporting becomes system-dependent. Important for banks, NBFCs, large corporates, and digital businesses.

CARO 2020

Companies Auditor's Report Order 2020

CARO 2020 replaced CARO 2016 and significantly expanded auditor reporting obligations. It applies to all companies except certain small private companies, banking companies, insurance companies, and Section 8 companies below threshold.

CARO requires auditors to report on 21 specific clauses covering fixed assets, inventories, loans, investments, statutory dues, borrowings, related-party transactions, fraud, and CSR — among others.

❖
Fixed Assets (Clause 1)
Whether the company maintains proper records of fixed assets, conducts physical verification periodically, and reports any material discrepancies found.
❖
Loans to Related Parties (Clause 4)
Compliance with Section 185 and 186. Auditor must report on terms and recoverability. Loans not at arm's length must be specifically flagged.
❖
Statutory Dues (Clause 7)
Report whether GST, PF, ESIC, TDS, customs, and income tax dues are regularly deposited. Amounts outstanding over 6 months must be specifically disclosed.
❖
Fraud Reporting (Clause 11)
Mandatory reporting of fraud by or on the company. Fraud above ₹1 crore must be reported to the Central Government. Cash losses in current and prior year must be disclosed.

"CARO 2020 transformed the auditor's role from a financial statement verifier to a governance watchdog — requiring detailed reporting on fraud, related-party transactions, whistle-blowers, and CSR compliance."

❖
Whistle-Blower Complaints (Clause 18)
Auditors must consider complaints received under the vigil mechanism (Section 177) and report on how management has handled them during the year.
❖
CSR Compliance (Clause 19)
Whether the company has complied with Section 135 CSR provisions — including whether unspent CSR amounts have been transferred to prescribed funds within deadlines.
❖
Undisclosed Transactions (Clause 1(vii))
Auditors must report on transactions not recorded in the books and whether they have been properly accounted for in the financial statements.

Whether the company has adequate IFC with reference to financial statements, and whether such controls operated effectively during the year.

Standards on Auditing

Key ICAI Standards on Auditing

Standards on Auditing (SAs) issued by ICAI govern how audits must be planned, executed, and reported in India.

📋

SA 200 — Overall Objectives

Defines the auditor's overall objectives: obtaining reasonable assurance that financial statements are free from material misstatement. Establishes the foundation of professional scepticism and audit judgment.

🔍

SA 315 — Risk Assessment

Requires the auditor to understand the entity, its environment, and internal controls to identify and assess risks of material misstatement at both the financial statement and assertion levels.

🛡️

SA 240 — Fraud Responsibilities

Addresses the auditor's responsibilities relating to fraud. Requires professional scepticism, specific fraud risk inquiries, and defined procedures when fraud risk factors are identified.

⚠️

SA 570 — Going Concern

Requires the auditor to evaluate management's going concern assessment. If material uncertainty exists, the auditor must report it — even if the financials are otherwise fairly presented.

📊

SA 540 — Accounting Estimates

Governs auditing of accounting estimates and fair value disclosures. Requires evaluation of management's estimation process, inputs used, and reasonableness of assumptions applied.

📄

SA 700 — Forming an Opinion

Governs the form and content of the auditor's report on complete general-purpose financial statements. Specifies the required elements of an unmodified (clean) audit opinion.

✏️

SA 705 — Modified Opinions

Covers circumstances where the auditor issues a qualified opinion, adverse opinion, or disclaimer of opinion — including specific language and placement of modifications in the audit report.

🤝

SA 600 — Using Work of Others

Governs group audit engagements involving component auditors. The group auditor retains full responsibility for the group audit opinion and must oversee component auditor work appropriately.

🔒

SA 505 — External Confirmations

Governs the use of external confirmation procedures — including bank confirmations, debtor confirmations, and legal representations. Covers design, execution, and evaluation of responses.

📋 Position Overview

We are looking for a detail-oriented and motivated Accounts Executive / Junior Accountant to join our growing team at V J Desai & Co. LLP. The ideal candidate is a B.Com / M.Com graduate or CA Intermediate student with 1–3 years of hands-on accounting or CA firm experience. You will work across client bookkeeping, GST compliance, TDS workings, and accounts finalization — under the direct guidance of our CA partners. This is an excellent opportunity to build deep practical expertise in a high-quality, structured professional environment serving 35+ industry sectors.

💼 Key Responsibilities
  • Maintain and manage day-to-day bookkeeping for multiple clients using Tally ERP / Tally Prime
  • Prepare and file monthly GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) and annual returns (GSTR-9 / 9C)
  • Perform bank reconciliation, debtor/creditor reconciliation, and detailed ledger scrutiny
  • Process TDS deductions, prepare quarterly returns (Form 24Q / 26Q), and generate Form 16 / 16A
  • Assist in finalization of accounts — trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet, and schedules
  • Prepare client MIS reports, cash flow statements, and profitability summaries
  • Support senior CAs in statutory and tax audit — vouching, ledger checking, working papers
  • Handle client correspondence — drafting letters and replying to GST/IT notices under CA supervision
🧩 Required Skills & Knowledge
  • Tally ERP / Prime: Data entry, ledger creation, voucher posting, and report generation
  • GST Compliance: GST returns, ITC reconciliation (GSTR-2A/2B), annual return filing
  • TDS & Income Tax: TDS sections (194C, 194J, 194I), return filing on TRACES/TIN portal
  • MS Excel: Pivot tables, VLOOKUP-based reports, and reconciliation sheets
  • Accounts Finalization: Final accounts with schedule-wise reporting
  • Communication: Professional email drafting, client coordination in English and Gujarati
🎓 Qualifications & Experience
  • Bachelor's degree in Commerce (B.Com) from a recognised university — mandatory
  • M.Com, MBA (Finance), or CA Intermediate is an added advantage
  • 1–3 years of practical experience in a CA firm, accounting firm, or accounts department
  • Prior experience with GST return filing and Tally ERP is strongly preferred
  • Freshers with strong Tally / Excel skills and excellent academic record may also apply
  • Fluency in Gujarati preferred; English proficiency is mandatory for all candidates
  • Ahmedabad-based candidates preferred; willing-to-relocate candidates welcome
💰 Compensation & Benefits

  • Annual performance-based increment reviewed directly by senior partners
  • Exposure to 35+ industry sectors — manufacturing, real estate, trading, services, and NGOs
  • Direct access to CA partners — learn from professionals with 30+ years of experience
  • Structured growth path: Accounts Executive → Senior Accountant → Team Lead
  • CA study support — candidates pursuing CA Inter/Final are encouraged and supported
  • Professional reference letter and experience certificate on successful tenure completion
