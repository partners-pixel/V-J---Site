Our GST Audit Services

GST Audit Services We Provide

📋

Pre-Audit Readiness Review

We examine your books, returns, and reconciliations (including GSTR-9/9C) to identify areas needing attention before any formal audit.

🏛️

Departmental Audit Support

Assistance during audits by tax authorities, including document preparation, on-site coordination, and response drafting.

📊

Statutory Audit Assistance

Support in preparing audit reports and reconciliations as required by law, even if CA certification is no longer mandatory, to ensure accuracy.

🔬

Special Audit Advisory

Guidance when a special audit is ordered, collaborating with appointed auditors and providing clarifications on complex issues.

🔍

Limited Scrutiny Handling

Helping with targeted compliance checks by the department, responding to notices and supplying requested information.

✅

Voluntary Compliance Audit

Internal review of compliance on your request to uncover issues in advance and mitigate risk.

⚖️

Post-Audit Resolution

Support in resolving audit findings, preparing replies to notices under Section 73/74, and implementing corrective measures.
