RERA Overview

Understanding RERA

The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA) was enacted to bring transparency, accountability, and efficiency to the real estate sector. It protects homebuyers' interests and establishes a regulatory authority in each state.

📌

Who Must Register

Promoters with projects having plot area >500 sq.m. or more than 8 apartments must register before advertising, selling, or booking.

📅

Registration Process

Application to state RERA authority with project details, land title, approvals, financial projections, and promoter details. Gujarat RERA operates at gujrera.gujarat.gov.in.

💰

Escrow Account

70% of funds received from allottees must be deposited in a separate escrow account and used only for land and construction costs of that project.

📋

Agent Registration

Real estate agents must register with RERA before facilitating any sale or purchase of registered projects. Separate registration for each state.

"RERA has fundamentally changed how real estate projects must be managed, compliance is not optional; it is the foundation of every project's legal standing."

V J Desai & Co. LLP, RERA Advisory
Promoter Obligations

Key Compliance Requirements

🗓️

Quarterly Updates

Promoters must update the RERA portal every quarter with construction progress, approvals obtained, and funds utilised, within 15 days of quarter-end.

📄

Annual Financial Disclosures

Audited financial statements, CA-certified statements of funds received and utilised must be uploaded annually for each registered project.

🔄

Project Extension

Extension beyond the registered completion date requires application to RERA with valid reasons. Force majeure grounds are available but require documentation.

📢

Advertisement Compliance

All advertisements must include the RERA registration number. Misrepresentation in ads is an offence under Section 89 of RERA.

🤝

Agreement for Sale

Promoters must execute a registered Agreement for Sale before receiving more than 10% of the unit price. Template prescribed by state authority must be followed.

🏗️

Structural Defect Liability

Promoters are liable for structural defects for 5 years after possession. Any defect must be rectified within 30 days of being brought to notice.
