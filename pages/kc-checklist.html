GST Compliance Checklist

GST Monthly & Annual Checklist

Monthly Compliance

☐
GSTR-1 filing by 11th
Upload all B2B and B2C invoices, credit/debit notes, and amendments for the month.
☐
GSTR-3B filing by 20th
Reconcile GSTR-2B, verify ITC eligibility, compute tax liability, and make payment before filing.
☐
ITC reconciliation
Match purchase register with GSTR-2B. Identify unclaimed ITC and follow up with vendors for non-filers.
☐
E-way bill review
Ensure all consignments above ₹50,000 had valid e-way bills. Archive expired bills with delivery proof.
☐
Verify RCM liability
Identify RCM-applicable purchases (GTA, legal services, imports) and ensure payment and ITC claim.

Annual Compliance

☐
GSTR-9 annual return
Reconcile all monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B data with books. File by 31st December.
☐
GSTR-9C reconciliation
If turnover > ₹5 crore, prepare and certify reconciliation statement. File with GSTR-9.
☐
Reversal of ITC
Identify ITC reversal required for exempt supplies (Rule 42/43), blocked credits, and non-payment to vendors within 180 days.
☐
HSN summary
Ensure HSN-wise summary is correctly reported in GSTR-9. Mandatory for all taxpayers from FY 2021-22 onwards.
☐
E-invoice compliance review
Verify all B2B invoices were generated through IRP (if applicable). Check IRN availability for all invoices.
Income Tax Checklist

Income Tax Compliance Checklist

TDS Compliance

☐
TDS deduction on all applicable payments
Verify TDS has been deducted at correct rates on salary, contractor, rent, professional fees, and other specified payments.
☐
TDS deposit by 7th of next month
Deposit TDS deducted in previous month. March TDS to be deposited by 30th April.
☐
Quarterly TDS returns (24Q/26Q)
File Form 24Q (salary) and 26Q (non-salary) within 31 days of quarter-end. Q4: by 31st May.
☐
Issue TDS certificates
Provide Form 16 (salary) by 15th June and Form 16A (non-salary) within 15 days of return filing.

ITR & Tax Planning

☐
Advance tax instalments
Pay 15% by June 15, 45% by September 15, 75% by December 15, 100% by March 15.
☐
Gather investment proofs
Collect 80C, 80D, HRA, LTA, and other deduction proofs. Submit to employer for accurate Form 16.
☐
Reconcile Form 26AS & AIS
Verify all TDS credits, high-value transactions, and income reported in 26AS and Annual Information Statement before filing ITR.
☐
File ITR by due date
Individuals/HUF: 31st July. Tax audit cases: 31st October. Transfer pricing cases: 30th November.

RERA Checklist

RERA Developer Compliance Checklist

☐
Project registration before launch
Obtain RERA registration number before advertising, selling, or accepting any booking amount for the project.
☐
Escrow account opened and maintained
Deposit 70% of funds received in designated escrow account. Use only for land cost and construction of that project.
☐
Quarterly update filed within 15 days
Upload construction progress, approvals, and fund utilisation on RERA portal within 15 days of every quarter-end.
☐
Annual CA certificate uploaded
Submit audited financial statements and CA-certified escrow fund utilisation statement annually for each project.
☐
RERA registration number on all ads
Ensure RERA registration number is prominently displayed on all advertisements, brochures, and digital marketing.
☐
Agreements for sale executed
Execute registered Agreement for Sale before collecting more than 10% of unit price from any allottee.
☐
Agent registration verified
Ensure all real estate agents facilitating project sales hold valid RERA agent registration certificates.
