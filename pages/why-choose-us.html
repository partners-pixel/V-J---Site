Our Story

Founded on Excellence Since 1993

The firm M/s. V. J. Desai & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants, was founded and established by Senior Partner CA Vinod J. Desai in 1993. At present, the Partnership firm has a team of Chartered Accountant Partners headed by a senior Chartered Accountant, supported by qualified, semi-qualified, and other staff personnel.

Since the setting up of practice, the firm has provided different professional services to companies, Government organizations, Trusts, and other diversified businesses in the private sector as well as to individuals.

There are other young & energetic partners who are Chartered Accountants with post-qualification experience of more than 10 years, specialising in Auditing, financial and loan consultancy, Income Tax, Service Tax, GST, RERA, and other related laws.
