Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

Answers to the questions we hear most often from new clients reaching out for the first time.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes — completely free and without any obligation. We use this time to understand your situation and determine how we can help before any engagement is formalised.

How quickly will you respond to my enquiry?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within one business day. For urgent matters — GST notices, IT assessments, RERA deadlines — please call +91 98250 62515 for same-day assistance.

Do you serve clients outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. While our office is in Satellite, Ahmedabad, we serve clients across Gujarat and India for GST, direct tax, FEMA, and advisory services via in-person, video, or phone consultations.

What industries and entity types do you serve?

We serve 35+ sectors including real estate, manufacturing, trading, NGOs, and professional services — for Pvt. Ltd. companies, LLPs, partnerships, proprietorships, trusts, and HUFs.

Is my financial information kept confidential?

Absolutely. All client information is held in strict professional confidence under ICAI's Code of Ethics, which mandates confidentiality across all client engagements.

Can you help with a GST or Income Tax notice?

Yes — this is a core service. Whether it is a GST scrutiny notice, show cause notice, income tax assessment, or litigation matter, we assess and draft a professional, compliant response.

Do you offer retainer-based monthly CA services?

Yes. We offer monthly and annual retainer arrangements for GST compliance, TDS management, bookkeeping, statutory filings, and management consultancy — tailored to your business volume.

What documents should I bring to the first meeting?

Nothing required. A general overview of your business, turnover range, current compliance status, and the specific issue you need help with is sufficient to begin.
