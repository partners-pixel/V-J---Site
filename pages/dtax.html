Our Services

Direct Taxation Services

Comprehensive direct tax services covering compliance, planning, and representation, for individuals, corporates, HUFs, LLPs, and NRIs.

Advance tax planning, MAT/AMT computation, transfer pricing documentation (Form 3CEB), and ITR-6 preparation and filing with all required schedules and disclosures.

💡

Tax Advisory

Business structuring, capital gains planning, DTAA benefit optimisation, investment advisory for tax efficiency, legitimate strategies that withstand regulatory scrutiny.

📄

Income Tax

Return preparation and e-filing for all taxpayer categories, individual tax planning, TDS compliance, and representation before ITO, CIT(A), and ITAT in assessment and appeals.

🌐

NRI Taxation

Residential status advisory, NRI ITR filing, property transaction guidance (Section 195, Form 15CA/CB), DTAA benefits, and repatriation with FEMA/RBI compliance.

How It Works

Our Tax Engagement Process

A structured approach that starts with understanding your complete financial picture and builds a tax strategy that is compliant, defensible, and optimised for your situation.

01

Financial Profile Review

Understanding your income sources, entity structure, investments, and prior year tax positions to establish a complete picture.

02

Compliance Assessment

Reviewing existing filings, TDS compliance, advance tax positions, and identifying open assessments or notices.

03

Tax Planning & Advisory

Developing legitimate tax planning strategies for the current year, covering deductions, exemptions, timing, and structuring opportunities.

Continuous guidance on transactions, regulatory changes, and planning opportunities throughout the financial year.
