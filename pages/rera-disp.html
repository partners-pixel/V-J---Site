Our Services

RERA Dispute Services We Provide

Each service is customised to your situation. Whether it is one complaint or multiple projects, we provide a strategic, end-to-end solution so you can focus on your core concerns, not legal technicalities.

📋

Complaints Filing and Drafting

We assist in drafting and filing RERA complaints (Form A/M) for buyers claiming delays, non-refunds, or defects. For developers, we prepare legal responses (Form N/B) to buyer claims, aiming to avert penalties. Our drafts address all relevant facts and reliefs.

🏛️

Legal Representation

Our advocates appear before the RERA Regulatory Authority or Adjudicating Officer on your behalf. We present your case clearly, whether you are seeking compensation or defending a claim. We handle hearings and cross-examinations.

📂

Documentation Support

We help organise crucial documents: sale agreements, payment receipts, bank statements, buyer-builder correspondence, and any project brochures for misrepresentation claims. This ensures your case is supported by complete evidence during the hearing.

⚖️

Execution of Orders

If a RERA order is not complied with, we initiate execution proceedings under Section 40. We work with the Authority and banks to enforce payment of fines or refunds ordered by RERA, recovering funds on your behalf.

📜

Appeals and Tribunals

We manage appeals (Form L) against RERA orders within the 60-day window. Our team prepares and argues cases before the Appellate Tribunal to challenge or uphold RERA decisions.

🤝

Conciliation Support

We guide parties through RERA's Conciliation Forum for disputes. By facilitating amicable settlements, we often resolve cases faster than formal hearings, saving time and cost.

How It Works

Our RERA Dispute Process

This structured approach ensures no step is missed. We manage timelines such as appeals within 60 days and communicate updates so you are never in the dark.

01
🔎
Step 01

Case Assessment

We begin by understanding your issue. For buyers, we evaluate facts of delays, refunds, or defects. For developers, we review the complaint. We confirm applicability of RERA and discuss the objectives.

02
📝
Step 02

Strategy and Drafting

We decide which RERA form is needed (M/A for complaints, N/B for replies, L for appeal). We gather documents including allotment letter, agreement, and receipts, and draft the submission, clearly stating the dispute and relief sought.

03
📤
Step 03

Filing and Notices

We file the complaint or response online via the state RERA portal, paying any required fee. The Authority scrutinises the filing, then serves notice to the other party and schedules hearings.

04
🏛️
Step 04

Representation at Hearing

We attend hearings, presenting your case and evidence. If challenges arise, we respond promptly. We pursue a resolution, either an order from the RERA Authority or a negotiated settlement in conciliation.

05
⚖️
Step 05

Order and Execution

After hearings, the Authority issues a binding order. We ensure compliance: if the developer fails to pay awarded amounts, we initiate execution proceedings under Section 40 to recover funds. We oversee enforcement until completion.
