Our EXIM Policy Services

EXIM Policy Services We Provide

We support all facets of export-import compliance and strategy. Each service is tailored to your specific trade needs, keeping your business competitive and compliant in global markets.

📋

Licenses and Registrations

Obtaining and renewing necessary approvals such as IEC registration, RCMC for export councils, import licenses (restricted items), and duty free EPCG authorisations.

💰

Export Schemes and Incentives

Assessing eligibility, preparing documents, and filing applications for export incentive schemes including RoDTEP, FPS, VKGUY, SEIS/SFIS, and TMA to maximise refunds and benefits.

🚢

Customs and Shipping Advice

Guidance on shipping documentation, claiming duty drawback or RoSL/RODTEP refunds, and resolving customs queries.

🌐

FEMA Compliance

Structuring inbound and outbound investments to meet RBI and FEMA rules, including branch/liaison office setups, FDI approvals, and reporting (APR, FLA returns).

🔍

Export Audit and Compliance

Preparing for export audits (DGFT, RBI), maintaining statutory files, and handling queries on export obligations and foreign currency transactions.

🏦

Trade Financing

Advising on methods to finance imports and exports including letters of credit, export bills, buyer's credit, export packing credit, and strategies for currency risk management.

📡

Policy Updates and Advocacy

Interacting with government agencies on your behalf, staying ahead of policy changes, and ensuring smooth implementation of any new FTP provisions.
