Our Services

Quarterly Disclosure Services We Provide

Each service is customised to your project's scale and location. Whether you have one project or many, we provide pragmatic, compliant solutions lightening your compliance burden so you can focus on delivering the project.

📋

Quarterly Update Filing

We prepare and submit your RERA quarterly updates on time. This includes filling Forms with project status, buyer bookings, approvals, and timelines. We handle uploads on the state RERA portal and liaise with regulators to resolve any queries quickly.

🏦

Escrow and Fund Reporting

We set up and monitor your escrow account. We ensure 70% of buyer payments go into the escrow as required and track fund withdrawals certified by architects. We include detailed fund utilisation and expense breakdowns in your quarterly reports.

🏗️

Project Progress Reporting

We compile up-to-date construction and completion data. This covers the percentage completion of each block or floor, certified work done, and adherence to timelines. We also document any change in project scope or plans as required by RERA.

📅

Compliance Calendar and Reminders

We create a custom timeline of all RERA deadlines and requirements for your project. We track upcoming reporting dates and send reminders, helping you prepare early. This proactive scheduling avoids last-minute rushes and penalties for late filing.

💡

Regulatory Advisory

We advise on all ongoing RERA compliance matters. Whether it is adjusting disclosures for project changes, interpreting new guidelines, or handling communication with the authority, we keep you informed and compliant.
