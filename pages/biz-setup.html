Our Business Setup Services

End-to-End Business Setup Services

We provide end-to-end business setup services to ensure your operations are legally compliant and strategically structured.

🏢

Entity Structuring and Incorporation

We assist in choosing and forming the right business entity. Whether a Private Limited Company, Public Ltd Company, Partnership, or LLP, we handle name approval, drafting and filing of MoA/AoA/Partnership Deed, and secure your Certificate of Incorporation.

📊

Tax Registration and Compliance

We set up your tax framework from day one. We handle GST registration, Income Tax registration (including PAN/TAN/IT registration), TDS obligations, and professional tax. We prepare and file initial returns and advise on ongoing tax compliance.

📋

Licenses and Permits

We identify and secure all required licenses for your industry. This includes Shops and Establishment licenses, Import-Export Code (IEC) for traders, FSSAI registration for food businesses, trade licenses, and any sector-specific permits.

🏦

Financial and Banking Setup

We help you separate personal and business finances from the outset. Our experts assist in opening business bank accounts, merchant accounts, and obtaining finance facilities. We also advise on accounting systems and initial bookkeeping practices.

📈

Business Planning and Advisory

We work with you to craft detailed business plans and financial projections. This includes market analysis, revenue models, and budgeting, essential for attracting investors, securing loans, or guiding internal strategy.

👥

Legal and HR Framework

We help draft employee contracts, set up payroll systems (including PF and ESI registration), and ensure compliance with labour laws. We also advise on protecting your intellectual property and handling legal agreements.

🔄

Ongoing Support and Advisory

Our commitment does not end at incorporation. We offer continuous support for your growing business, from annual compliance filings and tax planning to advisory on expansion or restructuring.
