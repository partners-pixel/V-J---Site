Our Business Valuation & Restructuring Services

Each service ensures decisions are backed by rigorous analysis and tailored to your objectives.

📊

Business Valuation

Comprehensive valuations of your entire business or specific assets. We use industry benchmarks and cash-flow analysis to determine fair value for mergers, acquisitions, fundraising, or internal planning.

🤝

Transaction Advisory

Valuation support for deals: mergers and acquisitions, joint ventures, and private equity. We help set a fair negotiation ground by pinpointing synergies and risks.

💰

Investment & Fundraising Valuation

Supporting capital raises by valuing equity and advising on pricing based on growth prospects and comparable companies.

🏢

Asset & Property Valuation

Expert appraisal of real estate, machinery, or intangible assets (like goodwill) for sale, investment, or financial reporting purposes.

🔄

Corporate Restructuring

Redesigning company structure for efficiency and growth. Services include core vs. non-core analysis, subsidiary consolidation, or divestitures to focus on key competencies.

🌐

Strategic Joint Ventures & Alliances

Planning and structuring partnerships or tie-ups. We evaluate how merging operations or sharing resources can create value and oversee implementation steps.

✈️

Cross-Border Structuring

Restructuring for international operations: advising on setting up overseas subsidiaries or merging with foreign entities, including regulatory and tax optimization.

⚖️

Tax & Risk Optimization

Aligning structure to minimize tax burdens and legal exposure. For example, reorganizing in response to changes in law or to reduce exposure to liabilities.

📋

Change Management Consulting

Guiding the execution of your restructuring plan. We coordinate with stakeholders, manage timelines, and help communicate changes to investors and regulators.
