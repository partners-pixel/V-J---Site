Our Corporate Law Services

Company Law Services We Provide

Each service is designed to make corporate law straightforward, letting you handle business matters without legal hiccups.

🏢

Company Formation

Advising on the optimal legal structure (Private Limited, LLP, etc.) and managing registration. We prepare and file incorporation documents to get your business legally established.

🏛️

Corporate Governance

Setting up board committees, drafting shareholder agreements, and implementing best practices in transparency. We align your governance with industry standards and regulatory expectations.

🤝

Mergers and Acquisitions

Supporting every stage of M&A deals including due diligence, deal structuring, contracts, and regulatory filings. We ensure transactions are efficient, compliant, and meet your strategic goals.

🔁

Restructuring and Reorganisation

Planning and executing spin-offs, mergers, share buybacks, or debt restructuring. We navigate approvals and documentation to achieve a smoother, tax-efficient corporate structure.

📋

Compliance and Reporting

Managing your statutory obligations including annual returns, financial statements, board resolutions, and ROC filings. We keep track of deadlines under the Companies Act and other regulations.

📝

Contracts and Agreements

Drafting, reviewing, and negotiating corporate contracts including JV agreements, vendor contracts, and franchising. We ensure all agreements protect your interests and comply with applicable laws.

📁

Secretarial Services

Handling board meeting logistics, minutes, statutory registers, and resolutions. We maintain your company records and filings, reducing administrative burden and risk of non-compliance.

👥

Shareholder and Board Matters

Managing share issuances, transfers, and meetings. We advise on shareholder rights, help resolve disputes, and maintain strong stakeholder relations.

🔍

Legal Due Diligence

Conducting thorough legal reviews before major transactions. We identify liabilities or compliance gaps so you can address risks before finalising any deal.

⚖️

Dispute Resolution

Resolving conflicts between shareholders, directors, or other stakeholders. Through negotiation, arbitration, or litigation support, we protect your interests and resolve issues efficiently.

🌐

FDI and Regulatory Compliance

Ensuring foreign investment complies with RBI and FDI rules. We guide you through investment structuring, filings, and approvals for inbound or outbound transactions.

💡

IP Rights Protection

Registering and safeguarding trademarks, patents, and copyrights. We help protect your brand and innovations under Indian law.
