Our FEMA Services

Full-Suite FEMA & Foreign Exchange Services

Each service is delivered with an eye on your business objectives, helping you manage foreign exchange matters efficiently while staying fully compliant.

🔍

FEMA Compliance and Advisory

Comprehensive guidance to manage and minimise foreign exchange risk. Includes advising on reporting obligations such as Annual Performance Reports, ensuring correct remittance procedures, and interpreting FEMA regulations relevant to your transactions.

📋

Registration and Approvals

Handling applications for FDI approvals, ECB applications, and permissions for overseas investments. We guide NRIs and residents through remittance routes and RBI filings to ensure all inbound and outbound investments meet legal requirements.

📄

Cross-Border Documentation

Preparing and reviewing documentation for inward and outward remittances including trade payments, service imports, and royalty payments. We structure foreign investments and fund flows to comply with FEMA limits and file necessary reports such as the FLA return.

🌐

Foreign Investment in India

For foreign companies or NRIs investing in India, we facilitate the entire process. We help structure FDI proposals, obtain regulatory approvals under automatic or government routes, and advise on establishment of subsidiaries or joint ventures.

✈️

Overseas Investments and Fund Transfers

Assisting Indian businesses and NRIs looking to invest abroad. Whether setting up an offshore branch or buying property overseas, we explain capital account rules, support ODI applications, and ensure compliance with RBI guidelines.

💸

Remittances and Cross-Border Payments

From personal remittances such as education and medical support to business payments including imports and contractor fees, we ensure your money transfers follow FEMA rules. We advise on allowable limits, documentation, and currency regulations.

🛡️

FEMA Audit and Investigation Support

We help companies review their FEMA compliance internally. Our team conducts audits to identify gaps and correct them before RBI notice. If there are enforcement actions or queries from the RBI or Enforcement Directorate, we represent you and clarify issues.

⚖️

Dispute Resolution

In case of any FEMA-related conflict such as a contested FDI approval or a foreign exchange violation notice, we provide legal support. We prepare appeals, attend RBI or ED hearings, and negotiate on your behalf to resolve contravention penalties.
