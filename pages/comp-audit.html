Compliance Audit Services

Structured Compliance. Controlled Risk.
Audit-Ready Operations.

Regulatory requirements today cut across tax, corporate law, labour, data, and sector-specific frameworks. We help businesses bring these into a single, controlled system.

A compliance audit gives your business a structured and independent review of how effectively your operations align with regulatory requirements. At V J Desai and Co., we assess your policies, controls, and execution practices to identify gaps, prioritise risks, and recommend implementable corrections.

Our compliance audit approach focuses on identifying gaps, validating controls, and ensuring that your business is not just compliant on paper but in execution. The outcome is not just an audit report, but a clear compliance roadmap that reduces exposure, improves governance discipline, and supports regulatory readiness.
