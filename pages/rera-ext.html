How It Works

Our Project Extension Process

This step-by-step service ensures your project's deadline shifts are managed legally and with minimal friction.

🔎
Step 01

Initial Assessment

We review your project timeline, pending permits, and delay reasons. We determine if you qualify for a Section 6 (force majeure) or Section 7(3) extension, and any state-specific requirements such as fee schedules and portal formats.

📂
Step 02

Preparation

We gather proof of delay such as disaster reports and government letters, and compile technical reports including progress certificates and cost audits. For Section 7(3), we help prepare consent formats or a justification dossier. We also help arrange necessary expert certifications from architects, engineers, or CAs.

📤
Step 03

Application Submission

We draft and file the extension application on the RERA portal (or offline if needed). This includes new completion dates and all supporting documents. We ensure allotted fees are paid and confirm receipt of application.

🤝
Step 04

Follow-Up and Negotiation

Once submitted, we monitor your case. If the authority raises queries or imposes conditions, we respond promptly. We negotiate practical solutions like partial approval or mediator proposals as needed.

✅
Step 05

Extension Approval

When an extension is granted, we update your compliance calendar and advise on new milestones. We communicate the revised schedule to buyers and stakeholders. If an extension is refused, we consult on next steps including appeals or restructuring the project to protect your interests.
