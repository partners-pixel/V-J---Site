Our Services

RERA Compliance Services

End-to-end RERA compliance for real estate developers in Gujarat, from initial project registration through to dispute response support.

📝

Project Registration Compliance

Complete documentation, application preparation, and GujRERA submission. Document checklist review, project detail verification, and ongoing registration management.

📅

Quarterly Updates & Disclosure

Preparation and filing of quarterly progress reports with physical and financial completion status, fund utilisation disclosures, and booking status updates within the 15-day deadline.

⏳

Project Extension Regulations

Extension application drafting and filing, force majeure advisory, revised completion schedule preparation, and direct liaison with GujRERA authority for approval and follow-up.

🛡️

RERA Dispute Response Support

Comprehensive replies to buyer complaints, representation before the Adjudicating Officer, RERA Appellate Tribunal appeals, and settlement advisory to avoid prolonged litigation.

How It Works

Our RERA Engagement Process

A structured approach to RERA compliance, ensuring every deadline is met, every disclosure is accurate, and every dispute is managed with procedural discipline.

01

Project Intake & Assessment

Understanding your project structure, approvals, land title documents, and current RERA registration status.

02

Document Checklist & Review

Preparing and reviewing all required documents for registration or quarterly filings before submission to avoid deficiency notices.

03

Application Preparation & Filing

Accurate preparation and submission of registration applications, quarterly reports, or extension requests through the GujRERA portal.

04

Authority Liaison & Follow-Up

Direct communication with GujRERA authorities for query resolution, approval follow-up, and any required clarifications or corrections.

05

Compliance Calendar Management

Setting up and maintaining a project-specific compliance calendar ensuring no quarterly deadline or disclosure obligation is missed.

06

Dispute & Escalation Support

If complaints are filed, we prepare structured responses and represent your position before the Adjudicating Officer and Appellate Tribunal.
